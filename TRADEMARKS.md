# Names and trademarks

Bluestone is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored, endorsed, or authorized by
the owners of the companies, products, formats, or standards it names.

Names such as Yamaha, XG, Roland, GS, VST, ASIO, CLAP, MIDI, Apple, macOS, Core MIDI, Microsoft, Windows, WinMM,
Linux, ALSA, and Bitwig Studio appear in Bluestone's software and documentation only to identify those products and
standards: to say which instruments, formats, and platform interfaces Bluestone works with or was tested against,
and, in engineering documents, which products' workflows informed a design. A workflow comparison describes
inspiration; it does not mean that Bluestone is compatible with, derived from, or a substitute for that product.
Compatibility statements describe what Bluestone does; they are not certifications. All trademarks remain the
property of their respective owners. Bluestone includes no third-party logos, icons, or product artwork.

## Bluestone's own name and marks

"Bluestone", the Bluestone logo (the stone mark drawn by `StoneMark`), and the Bluestone banner identify this project
and the builds its maintainer publishes.

The [MIT License](LICENSE) covers Bluestone's code. It does not grant permission to use the Bluestone name or logo
to identify another product. You are welcome to fork, modify, and redistribute Bluestone under that license, but a
fork or modified build distributed to others must use its own name and logo, and must not present itself as
Bluestone or as endorsed by the Bluestone project. Saying factually that your work is based on Bluestone is fine.
Contributions to this repository keep the name, logo, and look consistent, as the
[design language guide](docs/design-language.md) describes.

For any other use of the name or logo, contact the maintainer as described under [Corrections](#corrections).
